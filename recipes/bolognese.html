Bolognese Recipe

Rich, savory, and deeply comforting, this classic Bolognese is the ultimate slow-simmered Italian meat sauce. Built on a aromatic base of finely diced vegetables and seasoned ground meat, the sauce gets its tender texture and signature depth from a gentle splash of wine, rich tomato paste, and a touch of milk. Perfect for a cozy weekend dinner, it slowly cooks until thick and velvety, beautifully coating every strand of pasta for a restaurant-quality meal at home.

Ingredients

  • Olive oil
  • Onion
  • Carrot
  • Celery
  • Ground beef (or beef and pork mix)
  • Salt
  • Black pepper
  • Dry wine (red or white)
  • Tomato paste
  • Crushed tomatoes (or passata)
  • Milk
  • Pasta (such as tagliatelle)
  • Parmigiano-Reggiano cheese

Steps

  1. Sauté finely diced onion, carrot, and celery in olive oil over medium heat until softened.
  2. Add ground beef to the pot, season with salt and pepper, and cook until browned.
  3. Pour in a splash of dry wine and simmer until reduced.
  4. Stir in tomato paste, crushed tomatoes, and a touch of milk.
  5. Lower the heat and gently simmer the sauce for at least 30 minutes, stirring occasionally.
  6. Boil your pasta in salted water until al dente.
  7. Toss the drained pasta directly into the sauce with a splash of starchy pasta water.
  8. Serve topped with freshly grated Parmigiano-Reggiano cheese.
