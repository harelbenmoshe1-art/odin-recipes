Chocolate cake recipe

Rich, ultra-moist, and deeply comforting, this classic chocolate cake is the ultimate treat for true chocolate lovers. Featuring a soft, velvety crumb topped with a smooth, glossy chocolate frosting, it balances deep cocoa flavor with just the right amount of sweetness. Easy to prepare yet undeniably indulgent, it is the perfect centerpiece for birthdays, celebrations, or an effortless weekend sweet treat.

Example result

Ingredients

  • All-purpose flour
  • Unsweetened cocoa powder
  • Granulated sugar
  • Baking powder
  • Baking soda
  • Salt
  • Eggs
  • Milk
  • Vegetable oil
  • Vanilla extract
  • Boiling water (or hot coffee)
  • Butter (for frosting)
  • Powdered sugar (for frosting)

Steps

  1. Preheat your oven to 350°F (175°C) and grease two round cake pans.
  2. Whisk together the flour, cocoa powder, sugar, baking powder, baking soda, and salt in a large bowl.
  3. Add the eggs, milk, oil, and vanilla extract, beating until smooth and combined.
  4. Stir in the boiling water or hot coffee to create a thin batter.
  5. Divide the batter evenly between the prepared pans.
  6. Bake for 30 to 35 minutes until a toothpick inserted in the center comes out clean.
  7. Allow the cakes to cool completely before frosting.
  8. Whip soft butter, powdered sugar, cocoa powder, and a splash of milk to make the frosting.
  9. Spread a layer of frosting between the cake layers and cover the outside completely before serving.
