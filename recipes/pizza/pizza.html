Pizza recipe

Crispy, bubbly, and topped with vibrant fresh ingredients, this classic homemade pizza delivers pizzeria-quality perfection right from your oven. Built on a golden, chewy crust with a slight charred blister, it features a bright tomato sauce infused with garlic and herbs, melted mozzarella that pulls with every bite, and a scatter of fragrant fresh basil. Simple yet indulgent, it is the ultimate customizable crowd-pleaser for fun weeknight dinners or relaxed weekend gatherings with family and friends.

Example result

Ingredients

  • Pizza dough
  • Olive oil
  • Tomato sauce
  • Garlic
  • Dried herbs (oregano or Italian seasoning)
  • Mozzarella cheese
  • Fresh basil
  • Salt
  • Black pepper
    •

    Steps

    1. Preheat your oven (and pizza stone, if using) to its highest temperature setting.
    2. Stretch or roll the pizza dough out into your desired size on a lightly floured surface.
    3. Drizzle olive oil over the crust and spread a thin layer of tomato sauce over the top, leaving a border for the crust.
    4. Season the sauce with minced garlic, dried herbs, salt, and black pepper.
    5. Scatter shredded or sliced mozzarella cheese evenly across the pizza.
    6. Bake in the preheated oven until the crust is golden-brown and the cheese is melted and bubbly.
    7. Remove from the oven and garnish with fresh basil leaves before slicing and serving.
